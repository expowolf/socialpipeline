## How they market themselves
- **Free tier plus SEO content:** Buffer, Publer, Metricool.
- **"Alternative to X" and "X pricing" SEO pages:** Blotato, Publer.
- **Affiliates and YouTube tutorials:** Repurpose.io.
- **GitHub and open-source community:** Postiz.
- **Sales teams and enterprise deals:** Hootsuite.

## Gaps you could exploit
1. **Flat, creator-first pricing.** Charging per channel punishes someone who posts to 9 platforms.
2. **Posts that reliably go out.** Missed posts are the #1 complaint across competitors. Offer clear status for each platform, automatic retries and alerts.
3. **Real per-platform adaptation.** Automatically adjust caption length, aspect ratio, hashtags and TikTok/Shorts details, instead of copying one caption everywhere.
4. **A free tier that includes all platforms.** Competitors leave out X and LinkedIn.
5. **Make publishing the core feature,** with AI as a helper. Users complain about AI quality elsewhere.
