# Research: multi-platform posting app for creators (Nov 2026)
Files: [01-competitors](01-competitors.md) · [02-build](02-build.md) · [03-financials](03-financials.md)

## Summary
1. The market is crowded (Buffer, Publer, Later, Metricool, Blotato…). Your openings are **posts that reliably go out**, **flat pricing for creators**, and **automatic per-platform adaptation**.
2. Platform **approvals are the critical path**. Until TikTok and YouTube review your app, every post it makes stays private. Apply on day 1.
3. Build the MVP on a **shortcut API** (Upload-Post or Late), which costs ~$400–800 to start and ~$70/month to run. You can switch to the platforms' own APIs later.
4. At $12/month, ~10 users cover running costs and ~225 replace your paycheck.
5. Hiring someone ($18k+) would take years of saving. Building it yourself costs **time** (300–500 hrs ≈ 3–5 months at your hours), not money.

## Biggest risks
Approval rejections · token expiry silently breaking scheduled posts · X's per-post fees · depending on the shortcut provider · users cancelling.

## Next 3 steps
1. Check the top 3 competitors' prices yourself, then sign up for Publer's and Buffer's free tiers and use them for a week.
2. Talk to 10 creators: what do they hate about their current tool?
3. Buy a domain and send the Meta and TikTok developer applications. They take weeks.
